MAAK

A material project rooted in South Chita, Aichi.

Growing cotton without pesticides.
Building a place around it.

MAAK grows cotton without agricultural chemicals and is creating a restored traditional-house facility. It is an attempt to reconnect material, place and the people who make things.

VARIEGATOR

A wearable
archive.

VARIEGATOR rewinds the memories held in cinema and pop culture, then turns them into garments with a vintage character. It is not merchandise. It is a way of wearing a story.

At the front line of Japanese IP and pop culture.
